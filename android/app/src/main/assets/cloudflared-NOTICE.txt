The Termux build is an Android PIE (ELF ET_DYN) linked against Bionic's
/system/bin/linker64, built with GOOS=android and CGO_ENABLED=1. Android can
execute it from nativeLibraryDir, and its cgo resolver uses Android's DNS
directly, so no hosts patch or launcher wrapper is needed. The binary is
otherwise unmodified.
